I welcome all new members to the Delhi Society of Hematology. The society holds regular quarterly meetings at various institutions. I hope all the members will actively participate and learn from them. An annual conference is held in the month of September each year.An oration was started last year which is awarded to an eminent hematologist. All these events will help keep the hematologists and the postgraduates abreast with new advances and practices in hematology.
